# BLC-C8: Business Lead Classification

A data analytics and machine learning project focused on business directory analysis, review classification, and category-based lead prioritization.

### Key Features
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Google rating and review analysis
- Review-based business classification
- Category-based lead prioritization
- Indian vs. Foreign business classification
- Business analysis dashboards

### Technology Stack
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Jupyter Notebook, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau.

### Dashboard Insights
- Business distribution by city and category
- Average ratings and review volumes
- Top business categories
- Customer rating distribution
- Key business performance indicators

### Objective
To transform raw business directory data into actionable insights and support data-driven business lead prioritization.
